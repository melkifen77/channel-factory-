## Full Script (TikTok, ~45s, ~130 words)

> I'll show you the single point I recommend to more patients than any other. It's called An Mian — 安眠, Ān mián. The name translates to "peaceful sleep." That's the literal meaning.
>
> Find the bone behind your ear. Slide your finger down and back, into the soft hollow where your skull meets your neck. You'll feel a small dip. That's it.
>
> Press firmly. Not hard — firm. Hold for 90 seconds each side. Breathe slowly through your nose. You'll feel a gentle ache. That ache is the point.
>
> I've had patients who'd been on sleep aids for years feel something the first night they tried it. Not everyone — but most.
>
> The mechanism? That hollow is where your vagus nerve runs closest to the surface of the skull. Pressing it shifts you out of fight-or-flight. My grandmother didn't have that vocabulary. She just had 40 years of watching it work.
