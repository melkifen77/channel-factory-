## Full Script (TikTok, ~40s, ~105 words)

> You wake up at exactly 3am. Not 2, not 4. Three. Mind racing. Can't fall back asleep.
>
> In Chinese medicine, 1am to 3am is Liver time — 肝, Gān. My grandmother called it liver fire rising. For 2,000 years, TCM taught that waking in that window meant your liver was working harder than it should at night — usually from stress you hadn't processed during the day.
>
> I dismissed this for 30 years of Western practice. Then I read the cortisol research. We now call it nocturnal cortisol dysregulation. Same observation. Different vocabulary. 2,000 years apart.
>
> Here's what matters: this isn't a sleep problem. It's a daytime stress problem showing up at night. If you keep trying to fix it at 3am, you'll keep failing. The fix happens between 6 and 9pm — before you lie down.
