# Dr. Wei — Hook & Format Library
*Seed version · Expand after each 10 pieces of content*

---

## Core Principle

Don't generate hooks from scratch. Recombine what already works.

Every script starts by selecting a **hook template** + **body structure** from this library, then adapting it to Dr. Wei's persona and the specific topic.

```
Signal + Hook Template + Body Structure + Dr. Wei Persona = Script
```

---

## Hook Templates

Each template has: the pattern, emotional trigger, and Dr. Wei adaptation.

---

### H1 — The Specific Pain Hook
**Pattern:** "[Exact symptom] — here's what's actually happening"
**Trigger:** Recognition + curiosity
**Performance basis:** "Why you wake up at 3am" documented as highest-engagement hook in sleep niche

**Dr. Wei examples:**
- "You wake up at exactly 3am. Can't fall back asleep. Here's what your body is telling you."
- "You sleep 8 hours and wake exhausted. That's not insomnia. It's something else."
- "Your mind races the moment you lie down. Here's the mechanism."

---

### H2 — The Revelation Hook
**Pattern:** "I've been [X] for 40 years. Here's what I stopped [doing/recommending]."
**Trigger:** Authority + curiosity + implied insider knowledge
**Performance basis:** Physician-reversal format consistently high on medical content

**Dr. Wei examples:**
- "I prescribed sleep medication for 20 years. Then I watched what happened to my patients 5 years later. I stopped."
- "I dismissed my grandmother's sleep remedies for 30 years. Then I ran out of other options."
- "After 40 years treating insomnia, here's the one thing I wish I'd said sooner."

---

### H3 — The Bridge Hook
**Pattern:** "[Ancient/Eastern concept] = [Modern/Western concept]. 2,000 years apart."
**Trigger:** Curiosity + intellectual superiority (viewer learns something surprising)
**Performance basis:** "ancient wisdom + modern science" validation format well-documented

**Dr. Wei examples:**
- "What Chinese medicine called 'liver fire rising at night' — Western science now calls cortisol dysregulation. 2,000 years apart. Same observation."
- "TCM mapped organ activity to 2-hour windows 2,000 years ago. We now call it the circadian rhythm."
- "My grandmother had a name for what you're feeling at 2am. So does modern neuroscience."

---

### H4 — The Age Paradox Hook
**Pattern:** "At [age], I [counterintuitive outcome]. Here's why."
**Trigger:** Aspiration + curiosity (how is this possible?)
**Performance basis:** Personal testimony + age paradox consistently high saves

**Dr. Wei examples:**
- "At 68, I sleep better than I did at 38. Here's what changed — and it wasn't medication."
- "Most people sleep worse as they age. I sleep better. Here's the difference."
- "I'm 68. I haven't taken a sleep aid in 12 years. Here's my actual routine."

---

### H5 — The Counterintuitive Truth Hook
**Pattern:** "Most people [common behavior]. That's why they [bad outcome]."
**Trigger:** Status anxiety + correction
**Performance basis:** "We vs them" format documented as highest-engagement in authority content

**Dr. Wei examples:**
- "Most people reach for melatonin. That's why it stops working in 3 months."
- "Most people try to sleep 8 hours. Most people over 60 shouldn't."
- "Most people treat insomnia as a sleep problem. It's almost always a cortisol problem."

---

### H6 — The Simple Technique Hook
**Pattern:** "[Specific action] for [specific duration]. [Surprising claim]."
**Trigger:** Curiosity + low commitment (sounds easy)
**Performance basis:** Tutorial format has highest SAVE rate across all sleep content

**Dr. Wei examples:**
- "Press here for 90 seconds before bed. My patients are always surprised it works."
- "Dim your lights at 9pm. That single change improved sleep in 80% of my patients."
- "Drink this tea my grandmother made. I ignored it for 30 years. I was wrong."

---

### H7 — The Real-Time Context Hook
**Pattern:** "[Recent event]. Here's what it means for your sleep."
**Trigger:** Urgency + relevance
**Performance basis:** Event → implication format drives high comment engagement

**Dr. Wei examples:**
- "Daylight saving just changed. Here's why your sleep will be disrupted for 2 weeks — and how to shorten that."
- "New study on melatonin dropped this week. What it actually says (vs. what headlines say)."
- "It's getting darker earlier. Your body notices. Here's how to work with it."

## Body Structures

Select one per video. Never mix more than one in a 30–45 second video.

---

### B1 — Bridge Structure (East meets West)
```
Hook (TCM concept name)
→ Brief TCM explanation (1-2 sentences, grandmother's framework)
→ Modern scientific equivalent (what the neuroscience says)
→ Practical implication (what to do with this)
→ Optional soft CTA
```

**Best for:** Pillar 1 content (The Bridge), acupressure tutorials

---

### B2 — Revelation Structure (What changed)
```
Hook (what I used to do/believe)
→ The moment I changed my mind (patient story or grandmother's notebooks)
→ What I found out (the mechanism)
→ What I do differently now
→ What you can try
```

**Best for:** Pillar 2 content (The Revelation), myth-busting

---

### B3 — Technique Structure (Here's exactly how)
```
Hook (specific action + surprising claim)
→ The point/technique/ingredient (named, with TCM name if applicable)
→ The mechanism (why it works — brief)
→ Exact instructions (step-by-step, visual)
→ What to expect ("most patients feel X within Y")
```

**Best for:** Pillar 3 content (The Technique), acupressure tutorials, highest save rate

---

### B4 — Age Insight Structure (What changes after 50/60)
```
Hook (age paradox or counterintuitive claim)
→ What actually changes in sleep after [age] (biological mechanism)
→ What most people do wrong (common mistake for this age group)
→ What actually works (specific, practical)
→ Personal example (Dr. Wei's own adaptation)
```

**Best for:** Pillar 4 content (The Age Insight), 40-65+ audience

---

### B5 — Contrast Structure (Most people vs. what works)
```
Hook (most people do X)
→ Why X doesn't work (mechanism)
→ What actually works (specific alternative)
→ The evidence (patient observation, not study citation)
→ One thing to try tonight
```

**Best for:** Myth-busting, product recommendation setup (Month 2+)

## Emotional Trigger Reference

Use exactly one primary trigger per video. Don't stack.

| Trigger | When to use | Hook types |
|---|---|---|
| **Recognition** | Viewer has exact symptom | H1 |
| **Curiosity gap** | Claim without immediate explanation | H2, H3 |
| **Aspiration** | Viewer wants the outcome | H4 |
| **Status anxiety** | Viewer is doing it wrong | H5 |
| **Low commitment** | Simple action, surprising result | H6 |
| **Urgency** | Time-sensitive or event-linked | H7 |

---

## Anti-Patterns (What Kills Performance)

| Pattern | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Opening with "Hi I'm Dr. Wei" | Loses TikTok in 2 seconds | Hook first, always |
| More than one idea | Viewer can't save/share a clear takeaway | One idea, full stop |
| Study citation before personal observation | Sounds like a lecture | "My patients" framing first |
| Generic CTA ("follow me for more") | Indistinguishable from every other account | Specific value CTA ("more on the 3am pattern tomorrow") |
| Over-hedging before the insight | Kills credibility and energy | One disclaimer max, at the end if needed |
| Mentioning products before Day 60 | Destroys the no-agenda positioning | Strict phase gate |

---

## Library Expansion Protocol

After every 10 pieces of content:
1. Pull performance data from Postiz analytics
2. Identify top 3 performers by saves + shares
3. Extract: what hook type? what body structure? what topic?
4. Add 2–3 new hook variations inspired by top performers
5. Mark underperforming templates with ⚠️ — test one more time, then archive

The library grows smarter with every post. This is the learning loop.
